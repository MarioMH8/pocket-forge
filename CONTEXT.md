# Pocket Forge

System-wide vocabulary shared by every context in this repo. Context-specific terms live in the `CONTEXT.md` files listed in [`CONTEXT-MAP.md`](./CONTEXT-MAP.md).

## Language

**Pack**:
A versioned, distributable bundle that a project selects as one unit. A Pack contributes content, assets, locales, extensions, and bindings for capabilities, and composes with other Packs.
_Avoid_: Plugin, package, mod, bundle
